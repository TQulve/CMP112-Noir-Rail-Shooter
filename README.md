# CMP112-Noir-Rail-Shooter

This game is a rail shooter consisting of 3 levels that take place across a 1950s - 1970s fictional city.
You play as a detective mowing down a bunch of mafiosos due to non-specific reasons, culminating in a confrontation in their hideout, a warehouse. Swap between a few different guns during the battle as you juggle an ammo economy during your rampage.

All assets and music found and used under creative commons.
